About Paperplane

PDF tasks, made simpler.

Paperplane brings everyday PDF tools together in one place. You can merge or split PDFs, optimize a PDF, convert PDFs to JPG, Word, or Excel, and create PDFs from JPG images or Excel workbooks.

Designed for your browser

Paperplane processes selected documents in your browser. The document conversion tools do not upload your files to a Paperplane server. An internet connection may be needed to load the website and its third-party browser libraries.

What to expect

Results depend on the source document. PDF-to-Word and PDF-to-Excel can extract selectable text, but scanned documents need OCR and complex layouts may need editing after conversion. Always review the result before relying on it.
