Free online PDF tools for everyday documents

Paperplane brings common PDF tasks together in one place. Merge PDF files into one document, split out selected pages, or optimize a PDF before sharing. You can also convert PDF pages to JPG images, combine JPG images into a PDF, export selectable PDF text to Word or Excel, or save Excel spreadsheets as PDF. Processing happens locally in your browser, so your documents are not uploaded to a server.

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Choose Merge PDF, select at least two files, and start the merge. The combined PDF downloads to your device.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

This PDF to Word tool extracts selectable text. Scanned pages are images and need OCR before their text can be converted.

Can I convert a PDF to Excel?

The PDF to Excel tool extracts selectable text and estimates rows and columns from its layout. Scanned PDFs need OCR, and complex tables may need cleanup in Excel.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. The PDF tools process files in your browser. The page needs an internet connection to load its image and conversion libraries.
